Your waifu shows up on every single tab. YouTube, Twitter, news sites, everything. She never leaves you.

Grab and drag her anywhere on the screen. Your position is remembered across pages and browser restarts.

How it works

Up and running
in 30 seconds

No accounts, no setup, no nonsense.

1

Install the extension

Click "Add to Chrome" from the Chrome Web Store. It's free and takes about 3 seconds to install.

2

Pick your character

Click the WaifuEngine icon in your toolbar. Browse the character gallery, heart your favorites, and click any character to activate her.

3

She appears on every tab

Your chosen character floats over every webpage. Drag her wherever you want, resize her with the slider, and show/hide with one click.
